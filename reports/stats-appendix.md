# Statistical scope

Five seeds measure training/optimization variability conditional on each fixed dataset and chronological split. Comparisons share each seed-specific pretrained backbone and identical forecast origins and observed target masks. Means and sample SDs are descriptive. Overlapping windows, target variables and horizons are not counted as independent repeats. No significance claims, p-values or unstable five-seed bootstrap intervals are reported. Relative effects are computed within matched seeds; raw MSE values are not pooled across domains. All 190 planned runs are included. State-change groups use validation tertiles of historical half-window mean/scale differences; missing test groups are omitted, not imputed. Group labels are descriptive and their valid-element counts are reported.
